Method

The Mix

  1. Pour lukewarm water into a large bowl. Add the yeast and mix until fully combined.
  2. Add the flour, salt, and sugar.
  3. Begin mixing with a sturdy spoon, slowly drizzling the olive oil into the center as you stir.
  4. Once stirring becomes difficult, wet your hands and continue mixing by hand until a rough, shaggy dough forms.

The Folds

  1. Cover the bowl and let the dough rest at room temperature for 20 minutes.
  2. Remove the cover and perform a series of "stretch and folds" (pulling the edge of the dough up and into the center) until the dough begins to tighten.
  3. Form the dough into a ball, cover, and rest for another 20 minutes.
  4. Perform a second round of stretch and folds (about 6 folds) until the dough feels firm and smooth.

Proof & Shape

  1. Cover and let the dough rest for 1.5 hours at room temperature.
  2. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured or oiled surface. Cut the dough by weight into individual balls (reference the per-ball weight in the recipe above).
  3. Place the dough balls into a baking pan or proofing box, leaving about 2 inches of space between them. Lightly spray the tops with cooking oil.
  4. Cover and let rest for 1 hour. Your dough is now ready for pizza!

Pro Tip: Cold Ferment

For better flavor development, stop after Step 8 (the second stretch and fold) and place the covered dough in the refrigerator overnight (up to 3 days).

When you're ready for pizza time, remove the dough from the fridge 2 hours prior. Divide into individual balls, cover, and let them rest at room temperature for those 2 hours before stretching.
